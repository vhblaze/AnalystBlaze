-----------------------------------------------------------------------
analystblaze-frame-engine.exe
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the standalone PresentMon Console Application, built and
published by Intel Corporation as part of the open-source
GameTechDev/PresentMon project (https://github.com/GameTechDev/PresentMon).
It captures GPU present events via ETW (Event Tracing for Windows) to
measure real, in-game frame time - the same technique used by most
serious PC performance tools. AnalystBlaze uses it unmodified, under the
filename above instead of its original one, for consistent naming
alongside the rest of this installation.
